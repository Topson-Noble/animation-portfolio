Elamigos Foodtruck Commercials

A compilation of the animated series I created for the Elamigos Foodtruck company's commercials.

Project Overview

This is a compilation of all the animated commercials I created for the Elamigos Foodtruck company, based in Alsamiya, Kuwait.

Technical Details

- Production was done entirely using Blender 3D.

Role & Contribution

- I collaborated with the Elamigos director to brainstorm on the concept for the commerical and created the storyboards that illustrate this concept during pre-production.

- I was solely responsible for every aspects of the production, including the creation of all the 3D Assets (characters, cars, props, environment, animations), rendering, and post-production works.

Outcome

- The animation format of the commercials greatly helped to grow the social media followers and engagements, as well as attract more customers to the business.
